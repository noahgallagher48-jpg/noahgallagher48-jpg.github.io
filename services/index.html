The problem, as it shows up

  • The strong frames exist, but nobody can find them when the newsletter is due.
  • The same dozen images carry every campaign while whole programs go unseen.
  • The donor book comes up in October, after the photography it needed was not made in July.

If that reads like your file server, the conversation is worth having.

The approach

Every engagement starts with one question: how do you want people to feel when they look at these photos? Not this or that program or activity. Most of what I capture is in between. Before the shoot we settle what the images need to do and what you already have. I make what is missing. The delivery comes back organized by use. On residencies, your media team gets a working session and a field guide. Selected sequences become campaign collections, donor books, calendars, fine-art prints, and gifts.

You're not photographing activities. You're photographing kids and counselors and members of the community engaging with the activity as a context for it.
