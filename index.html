Services

I work with organizations and individuals whose work depends on being seen and felt.

The residency

For camps, retreat centers, and other locations with rich before- and after-hours character, building or refreshing their visual identity. Three nights on-site to build a deep, deployable library that holds up across years and channels, delivered organized by use case with an implementation strategy guide.

The focused day

A focused day on-site producing a curated set of images for a specific need: a campaign, a viewbook update, a board meeting, a capital campaign launch, an event.

Personal branding

For facilitators, leaders, and individuals whose work depends on being seen clearly. I photograph you in the spaces where and when your work happens.

Media-team training rides along with camp engagements: a working session with your team and a field guide they keep, tuned to the gear your camp already owns.

About

My professional foundation was built navigating the realities of non-profit management. I always had a camera nearby, and I found myself pointing it at the institutions I served, capturing the authentic way those spaces actually felt. Those photos drove engagement, reignited nostalgia, and moved donors to support exactly what they were seeing.

I don't just understand lighting and composition; I understand stewardship, donor relations, and the metrics of a successful development office. When I partner with an organization, I am looking at your environment through the lens of a director who needs to prove the ROI of their mission.

Find the engagements. Find the connections. Photograph those.
