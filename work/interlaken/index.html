Camp Interlaken JCC · Eagle River, Wisconsin

Four days in residence, July 2026

The night the sky cleared, the camp cut its own lights for the stars.

A summer residency: on site from Shabbat to Shabbat's edge, shooting the camp's days and its nights, then delivering a library the whole team can work from.

A selection

Twelve frames the camp calls fine art.

How the camp is using it

The images are writing the campaign

Right now a committee is moving through the summer's photographs, marking favorites and answering one question about each: what does this bring back for you. Their answers are informing the language of Bridge to the Future, the camp's campaign.

The prints become donor gifts: the work on a supporter's wall, from the place their giving built.

What the camp received

The summer library
Every delivered photograph in one place, organized by what each image is for, in two sizes: full resolution for print, web for screens.
The field guide
A short guide written for the camp's own media team: what to shoot, how to shoot it, and why it matters. It stays behind after the residency ends.
Two hardcover book layouts
Designed to press spec and printed through the lab at cost.
The favorites ballot
A way for the camp's committee to choose together: every voice, one page, and the answers feed the campaign.
