Gluten free in Sandusky

The appendix for Susanna and Leo · September 4 to 7

Two of us eat gluten free, and cross-contact counts. This page is the short version of where that is easy, where it takes one sentence to the server, and what goes in the grocery cart. Nothing here replaces the ask at the counter.

The one sentence, every time: "Two of us are gluten free and cross-contact matters. Do you have a dedicated fryer, and can this come without the bun, croutons, or soy sauce?" A fresh pair of gloves and a wiped surface is a fair ask.

Sunday

Cedar Point

One stand is built for this, and the park lets dietary food through the gate. Those two facts are the whole plan.

  • The Wild Turnip is the dedicated gluten free stand: GF chicken tenders, GF pizza, sweet potato tots, its own fryer. Ask at the gate where it is; stands move between seasons. Lunch there at 11:15, before the noon crush.
  • Bring a small soft cooler. The park allows food for dietary or medical needs through security. Bars, fruit, cheese sticks, pretzels, jerky. Say "dietary" at the bag check and it goes through. The rest of the group's food stays in the car; the picnic area is by the parking lot.
  • Wagon Wheel has a GF crust but shares a kitchen. Backup only.
  • Everywhere else: assume every fryer is shared. Bunless burgers and hot dogs, grilled chicken, salads, and nothing fried unless the stand says it has a dedicated fryer. Chick-fil-A grilled nuggets are safe in themselves; ask whether the sealed GF bun is stocked. Melt may have GF bread; ask.
  • Guest Services, just inside the main gate, holds the allergen list for every stand and can call a kitchen. Worth two minutes if anything is unclear.
  • Ice cream in cups, Dippin' Dots, fresh fruit cups, and kettle corn are the safe treats. Funnel cake, elephant ears, and pretzels are not.
Friday, 2 PM

The grocery order

Meijer or Kroger on Milan Road, ten minutes south. Everything below is a common stocked brand at either. Labeled "gluten free" beats "wheat free" every time.

Bread and buns. Canyon Bakehouse or Udi's: hamburger buns (8), bagels (12), one sandwich loaf for the cooler. Schar if those are out.
Salty snacks. Snyder's or Glutino GF pretzels, Tostitos or Late July corn chips, Skinny Pop, Lay's Classic, Cheetos Crunchy, Simple Mills or Crunchmaster crackers, Lundberg rice cakes.
Bars and protein. Larabar, RXBar, KIND, Nature's Bakery GF fig bars, cheese sticks, hummus, hard-boiled eggs, jerky with a GF label (many use soy sauce).
Sweet. Gluten free Oreos, Tate's GF chocolate chip, Enjoy Life cookies, Hershey's bars, Jet-Puffed marshmallows, Pamela's or Kinnikinnick GF grahams for s'mores.
Breakfast. Rice Chex or Corn Chex, Cheerios, Bob's Red Mill GF oats, yogurt, fruit, butter and jam that stay in their own jars.
Pantry and grill. Corn tortillas for the taco bar, tamari instead of soy sauce, ketchup and mustard are fine, skip malt vinegar and any beer-based marinade. Glutenberg or Redbridge for a GF beer; cider and seltzer are safe.
House rules that cost nothing: a separate "GF" bag for buns and bagels, GF toast in the oven, their sandwiches made first on a wiped board, no double-dipping into the butter or peanut butter, their burgers on the grill first.
